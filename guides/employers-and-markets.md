# Where security teams sit

*Last reviewed: November 2026*

The same job title can feel like two completely different careers depending on who employs you and where you work. A security engineer at a 60-person software startup, a consultant at a global advisory firm and an analyst at a managed security service provider (MSSP) have different hours, breadth, career speed and day-to-day pressures.

## Employer types compared

| Employer type | What you gain | Common trade-offs | Paths with strong hiring |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MSSP or MDR provider** | Fast exposure to many environments and attack types; strong entry point for juniors | High alert volume, shift work, limited time to fix root causes | [Security operations](../paths/security-operations.md), [detection engineering](../paths/detection-engineering.md), [incident response](../paths/incident-response-forensics.md) |
| **Consultancy or Big 4** | Variety across sectors and clients; structured training; strong CV signal | Billable-hour pressure, travel or tight deadlines, you advise rather than own long-term outcomes | [Offensive security](../paths/offensive-security.md), [GRC](../paths/governance-risk-compliance.md), [privacy](../paths/privacy.md), [AI governance](../paths/ai-governance-assurance.md), [incident response](../paths/incident-response-forensics.md) |
| **In-house enterprise** | Ownership of systems over years; deeper context; generally predictable hours outside IR | Slower change cycles, legacy systems, more internal politics | [Vulnerability management](../paths/vulnerability-management.md), [IAM](../paths/identity-access-management.md), [security engineering](../paths/security-engineering-architecture.md), [GRC](../paths/governance-risk-compliance.md), [OT](../paths/ot-industrial-security.md) |
| **Tech or SaaS company** | Modern cloud and engineering stacks; high automation; strong compensation at senior levels | High technical bar; smaller teams mean wide scope and less formal training | [Application security](../paths/application-security.md), [cloud security](../paths/cloud-security.md), [detection engineering](../paths/detection-engineering.md), [AI security engineering](../paths/ai-security-engineering.md) |
| **Security vendor** | Deep technical peers; building products used across the industry; research opportunities | Narrower view of how buyers actually run operations; commercial pressures | [Vulnerability research](../paths/vulnerability-research.md), [threat intelligence](../paths/threat-intelligence.md), [detection engineering](../paths/detection-engineering.md), [AI red teaming](../paths/ai-red-teaming.md) |
| **Government, defence and critical infrastructure** | High-consequence mission; stability; exposure to nation-state threats and OT | Security clearances, citizenship or residency rules, slower hiring and lower base pay in some countries | [Incident response](../paths/incident-response-forensics.md), [threat intelligence](../paths/threat-intelligence.md), [OT](../paths/ot-industrial-security.md), [GRC](../paths/governance-risk-compliance.md) |

## Moving between employer types

A common career pattern is starting in an **MSSP or consultancy** for two to three years to build rapid breadth, then moving **in-house** to build and own a programme over time—or moving from in-house into a consultancy or vendor once you have deep operational stories to share.

## Regional differences to know about

Most core technical skills transfer globally, but hiring signals, frameworks and regulations vary by market:

- **United States:** Job descriptions frequently map to the [NICE Workforce Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/applied-cybersecurity/nice/nice-framework-resource-center). Federal and defence contractor roles often require specific certifications under DoD 8140 and national security clearances.
- **European Union:** Roles align increasingly with the [ENISA European Cybersecurity Skills Framework](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/topics/cybersecurity-education/european-cybersecurity-skills-framework). Regulation drives large demand in GRC, privacy and AI governance through the GDPR, NIS2, DORA (financial services) and the EU AI Act.
- **United Kingdom:** The [UK Cyber Security Council](https://www.ukcybersecuritycouncil.org.uk/) maintains a national career framework and professional registration routes. Consulting and offensive roles often reference CREST accreditation alongside international certifications, and boards frequently use [NCSC](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/board-toolkit) guidance.
- **India:** One of the largest security hiring markets globally, split across global capability centres (GCCs) for multinational banks and tech companies, major IT services and consulting firms, and fast-growing SaaS startups. Demand is strong across SOC, IAM, cloud security, AppSec and GRC (including CERT-In reporting obligations and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act).
- **Australia and Singapore (APAC):** Australia's market leans heavily on the [Australian Signals Directorate's ACSC](https://www.cyber.gov.au/) Essential Eight (evolving into the ASD Essentials from October 2026) and IRAP assessments for government and regulated sectors. Singapore's [Cyber Security Agency (CSA)](https://www.csa.gov.sg/) and financial regulators drive strong demand in regional banking, cloud security and OT.
