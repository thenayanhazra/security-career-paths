# Certifications by path and level

*Last reviewed: November 2026. Confirm exam versions, prices and prerequisites on the issuer's site before booking.*

Certifications help in two ways: they get your CV past automated screening, and they give your study a structure. They do not replace practical skill, and employers who know the field can tell the difference within minutes. A good rule: **one entry-level certification to get noticed, then hands-on work, then certifications that match the job you want next.**

Levels below are relative. "Starting" means achievable without security experience. "Building" assumes a year or two in the field. "Advanced" usually needs several years, and some formally require them.

## What changed in 2026

- **ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) is no longer free.** ISC2 closed its One Million Certified in Cybersecurity programme to new enrolments on 20 May 2026. Exam codes already issued under it can be used until 31 December 2026.
- **CompTIA Security+ is changing version.** SY0-701 is the current exam; its successor, SY0-801, is due around the end of 2026 and adds AI and LLM content. Both lead to the same Security+ certification, so study for whichever version you can sit.
- **AI security certifications arrived in number.** CompTIA launched SecAI+ in February 2026. GIAC released AI Platform Security (GAIPS) and AI Security Automation Engineer (GASAE). ISACA extended its AI series (AAISM, AAIA, AAIR). ISC2 announced an AI security certification in July 2026 with a pilot exam expected late in the year.

## Starting out (any path)

| Certification | Issuer | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Security+](https://www.comptia.org/en/certifications/security/) | CompTIA | The most widely requested entry-level security certification. Vendor-neutral, broad. |
| [Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cc) | ISC2 | Lighter than Security+. No longer free from May 2026. |
| [Network+](https://www.comptia.org/en/certifications/network/) | CompTIA | Worth it if networking is your weakest area. |
| [Google Cybersecurity Certificate](https://grow.google/certificates/cybersecurity/) | Google, on Coursera | A structured course with a certificate rather than a proctored exam. Good for career changers who want guided study. |

## Prerequisites to know about

- **CISSP** requires five years of paid work in at least two of its eight domains (four with a relevant degree or certain other certifications). You can pass the exam earlier and hold "Associate of ISC2" status until you have the experience.
- **CISM, CISA and CRISC** each require five years of relevant experience, verified within a set window after passing.
- **AAISM** requires an active CISM or CISSP. **AAIA** builds on CISA and **AAIR** on CRISC.
- **SecAI+** has no formal prerequisite, but CompTIA recommends three to four years in IT, including two in security.

## Spending wisely

- Many employers pay for certifications once you are hired. Ask before paying yourself.
- Free and low-cost training exists for almost every exam. Expensive bootcamps are rarely necessary.
- Certifications expire or need continuing education credits. Factor in renewal fees before collecting many.
- Government and defence roles sometimes require specific certifications. In the United States, check the DoD 8140 qualification matrix for the role you want.
