# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this guide to be a welcoming, accurate and useful resource for everyone—from complete beginners asking their first question to senior practitioners sharing hard-won lessons.

We pledge to treat every contributor and reader with respect, regardless of experience level, background, age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, nationality, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- **Be constructive and specific.** When suggesting a change or pointing out an error, focus on the facts, the source and how to improve the page.
- **Remember what it felt like to be new.** Questions in Discussions or Issues from beginners deserve patient, clear answers without gatekeeping or condescension.
- **Keep recommendations honest.** Disclose affiliation when suggesting your own tool, course or article, and avoid spam or purely promotional links.
- **Respect regional and career diversity.** Security work looks different across countries, company sizes and sectors; treat differing experiences as perspective rather than mistakes.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, insults or derogatory comments
- Gatekeeping or mocking beginners for what they do not yet know
- Publishing private information about others without permission
- Repeated spam, affiliate links or unsolicited commercial promotion
- Anything that encourages unauthorised testing or illegal activity

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues, discussions or pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban repeat offenders from the repository.

If you see behaviour that violates these standards, please contact the repository maintainer directly through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/thenayanhazra) or open a private report if available. All reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.
